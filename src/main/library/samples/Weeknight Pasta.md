# Weeknight pasta

Boil a large pot of salted water. Cook 200 grams of spaghetti until just tender.

While it cooks, warm olive oil in a wide pan with two sliced garlic cloves and a pinch of chili. Add a drained tin of tomatoes and simmer for eight minutes. Toss the pasta with the sauce, a splash of pasta water, and a handful of basil.

Serve with parmesan. This is enough for two people on a Wednesday, with bread if you have it.
