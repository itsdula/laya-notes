# Tokyo weekend

Land at Haneda on Friday evening and take the monorail into the city. The hotel is a short walk from a subway station in Yanaka.

Saturday is a slow walk through the neighborhood, a long lunch, and a bookstore that stays open late. Sunday morning is the teamLab museum, then the flight home.

Pack a light coat. The forecast for late October is cool, and the evenings are clearer than the afternoons.
