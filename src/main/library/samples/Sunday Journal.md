# Sunday journal

I woke before the alarm and made coffee while the street was still quiet. The plan for the day is small: laundry, a walk along the river, and a phone call with my sister.

I have been thinking about how little of the week I actually remember. Writing a few lines in the evening is the only way the days do not blur together.

Tonight I am grateful for the early dark, and for the fact that nothing in the house is broken.
